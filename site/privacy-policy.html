Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Elemental Inventory app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Daniel Kissel.

Information Collection and Use

The app collects device data including your device's Internet Protocol address, visit patterns, time spent using the app, and the mobile operating system you use.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The application may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery, with consent obtained where legally required.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting elementalinventoryapp@gmail.com.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what data is collected, request deletion, opt out of the sale of personal data, and receive protection from discrimination for exercising these rights. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Third-Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services. Third-party providers used by the app include Google Play Services, AdMob, and Sentry, which receives crash and error reports (such as error details and your device model and operating system version) so bugs can be found and fixed.

International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred internationally using Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or user consent where applicable.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the app by uninstalling it. Previously transmitted data is not automatically deleted.

Data Retention Policy

User-provided data is retained for 12 months after last use; automatically collected data is retained for 24 months; aggregated data may be retained indefinitely.

Children

The application is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information.

Data Breach Notification

A breach affecting personal data will be handled with notification in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; updates will be posted here with their effective date, and material changes will require your consent where required by law.

Your Consent

By using the app, you consent by affirmatively opting in, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at elementalinventoryapp@gmail.com.
